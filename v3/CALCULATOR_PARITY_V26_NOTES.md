# Quotation V26 — Calculator parity

- Solution quotation follows the same 12/09/2026 policy structure as Deal Calculator: 4/6/8 sessions, start-month proration, same-unit multi-site support fee, module minimum, training blocks, assessment, and equipment recovery.
- Standard equipment module = 4 ROBOT + 2 MAP + 2 OBSTACLE + 1 CARDS + 1 BOX + 1 BRAND_DECOR_FORMEX = 31.7m.
- Required modules = max(scale modules, deployment sites); >800 children is blocked for custom approval.
- Admin may change component quantities after inspection. Quantity below standard requires a written reason (existing/compatible equipment or approved exception).
- If school buys equipment, actual component quantities are chargeable lines at catalog prices.
- If Sunbot finances equipment, recovery is calculated from actual configured equipment capital × 1.30 / 24 or 36 months, and only months remaining in current school year are charged in the current-year quotation. Residual continues after May.
- General discount only affects PROGRAM_FEE_FORMULA. Equipment, site support, training and assessment remain governed separately.
- Non-policy add-on lines are preserved when recalculating a solution.
- Retail / repair is intentionally untouched and remains Admin-only through the existing RETAIL_REPAIR flow.
